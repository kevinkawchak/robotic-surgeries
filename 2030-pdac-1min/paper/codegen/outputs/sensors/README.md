This directory contains the publication grade sensor outputs for the v0.6.0 PDAC 1 minute 8 arm Whipple simulation.

## Files

- `sensor_sample_8arm.csv`: 81 columns (1 tick + 80 channels) by up to 1001 rows. Arm 1, Phase 5 pancreaticojejunostomy stitch sub task, first 100 milliseconds. The committed file shows the schema with three representative rows; the full file is regenerated by `python -m src.sensors.ingest_8arm --arm-id 1 --duration-ms 100`.
- `per_arm_summary.csv`: Per arm per channel summary statistics (min, max, mean, std, p95) across all 32 iterations.

## Channel Inventory

The 80 per arm channels follow the schema at `../../schemas/sensor_record_8arm.schema.json`. The PDAC specific channels include:

- `vessel_proximity` (100 kHz, 0.01 mm resolution): distance to nearest active vessel surface in the per phase active vessel set.
- `nir_icg_*` (10 kHz, 4 channels): NIR indocyanine green at 800 nm for vessel and bile leak verification.
- `duct_manometry` (10 kHz, 0.1 mmHg): pancreatic duct pressure during Phase 5.
- `ring_tension` (10 kHz, 0.01 N): anastomosis ring tension during Phases 5 6 7.
- `bile_spectrophotometry` (10 kHz, 4 channels at 410 470 532 600 nm): bile leak detection during Phase 6.
- `us_b_mode` (10 kHz, 8 element array): ultrasound B mode for vessel mapping and margin scan.
