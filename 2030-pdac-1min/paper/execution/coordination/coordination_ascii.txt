10 kHz Heartbeat Bus - 8 Arm Broadcast Cycle
=============================================

  T (microseconds, 0 to 100 us per 10 kHz tick)
  0         12        24        36        48        60        72        84        100
  +---------+---------+---------+---------+---------+---------+---------+---------+----+
  | Arm 1   | Arm 2   | Arm 3   | Arm 4   | Arm 5   | Arm 6   | Arm 7   | Arm 8   | RX |
  | 32 B    | 32 B    | 32 B    | 32 B    | 32 B    | 32 B    | 32 B    | 32 B    |16us|
  +---------+---------+---------+---------+---------+---------+---------+---------+----+

  Per arm 32 byte response frame layout:
    bytes 0   to 3   : heartbeat_seq (uint32)
    bytes 4   to 7   : watchdog_deadline_us (uint32)
    bytes 8   to 11  : tip_force_scalar (float32)
    bytes 12  to 15  : cumulative_cross_arm_force (float32)
    bytes 16  to 19  : force_time_integral (float32)
    bytes 20  to 23  : phase_id + arm_id + estop_state + collision_state (packed)
    bytes 24  to 27  : engagement_depth (float32)
    bytes 28  to 31  : reserved (CRC32 + sequence ack)

  100 us watchdog deadline:
    if a master to arm tick is not acknowledged within 100 us, the arm
    raises a local e stop and broadcasts a recovery frame on the next tick.

  3 ms cross arm e stop budget:
    on any per arm e stop, the master broadcasts an e stop to all 7 other
    arms within 3 ms across the 10 kHz bus.

  50 us per arm park budget:
    after e stop receipt, each arm parks in 50 us by ramping
    target_linear_vel to 0 over 5 ticks.
