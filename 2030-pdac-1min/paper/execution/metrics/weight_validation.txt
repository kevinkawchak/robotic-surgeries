Note on the codegen smoke test discrepancy:
  The codegen smoke test at tests/test_smoke.py asserts that the
  PancreSpeed composite equals 93.55 and that the Dutch human
  baseline composite equals 56.05. The deterministic output of the
  frozen weights applied to the baseline component scores yields
  93.75 and 67.90 respectively. The discrepancy is a known issue
  in the v0.6.0 codegen and is documented at:
    2030-pdac-1min/paper/execution/tests/test_status.txt

  The execution tree uses the deterministic output (93.75, 67.90)
  rather than the test target (93.55, 56.05) because the
  deterministic output is reproducible from the released weights.
