On premises repository based large language models (LLMs) provide commands to standard oncology surgical robots based on real time sensor data and are controlled via x, y, z coordinates to administer patient treatment, which minimizes single robot error potential. The thesis is operationalized through a four phase Claude Code workflow (instructions plus codegen plus execution plus draft paper) producing a 60 second 8 arm robotic pancreaticoduodenectomy on a hypothetical 2030 Medtronic PancreSpeed 1.0 platform paired with the Daraxonrasib pan KRAS multiselective inhibitor (FDA Breakthrough Therapy 06/25, RASolute 302 plus RASolve 301). PancreSpeed 1.0 closes a 5x to 500x gap against 2025 SOTA (1,200 mm/s tip, 100 kHz force, 0.05 mm RMS, 3 ms E stop, 18 N cumulative tip force cap, 640 channel sensor stack at 80 channels per arm). A 32 iteration deterministic Latin hypercube sweep at seed 20260513 yields mean composite 93.298 (std 1.225, 95\% CI half width 0.462), and a 4 entrant LLM tournament places PancreSpeed 1.0 first at 93.735 over hypothetical 2030 da Vinci SP and Hugo RAS successors and the 2025 Dutch human baseline (ideal outcome 47.0\%). The 1001 record Phase 5 first 100 ms `sensor_sample_8arm.jsonl` is the largest exceptional processing feat. Daraxonrasib postop restart distributed as T+7d in 29 of 32 iterations and T+14d in 3 of 32. The practical adoption gap remains synthetic patient, hypothetical hardware, and TRIPOD+AI plus CREMLS reporting floors under \S 1.4 and \S 4.5.
