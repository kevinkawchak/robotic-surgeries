# Section 2: Methods

Performant conversational AI models were used in this study to address software disadvantages with text protocols supporting code, biopharmaceutical variables addressed by imported settings from a prior QSP study, and ease of use english conversations throughout the project with state-of-the-art AI. AI models were further used in this study to process the FDA Assessing the Credibility and M15 documents to produce test recommendations executed by the user throughout the study. Additionally, this current study's abstract utilized Appendix 1 elements of MIDD and ASME V&V 40 in accordance with FDA compliance.

Digital twin arms were based on a prior paper's Daraxonrasib + Mitazalimab + liposomal Irinotecan combinations with Control nal-IRI+5FU control; and additional arms of Magrolimab and MRTX-1133 with Gemcitabine + nab-Paclitaxel control were from the most recent Quantitative Systems Pharmacology (QSP) study. Baseline characteristics, archetypes, and trial timelines were utilized from the two most recent trial simulations. A quad AI notebook peer review of the author's previous QSP trial was performed using ChatGPT, Gemini, Opus, and Grok. Additional digital twin trial structure and patient log file structures were derived from user instructions and the author's prior QSP model as a template using ChatGPT 5 Pro Research (ChatGPT), shown in Standard 1 Prompt 01. Further adaptations through iterative prompting to obtain a plain text trial protocol were also obtained with ChatGPT. The first functional Python notebook was obtained by Opus 4.1 Extended (Opus) using the text protocol, and prior QSP final notebook, shown in Prompt 03. Parameter changes were made utilizing AI, with results of prior notebooks utilized as inputs to further improve the developing notebook. External validation of control arms A and G from literature were incorporated based on AI online literature searches and included in prompts to aid the calibration process, shown in Prompt 05. See Data availability for file names.

Additional notebook optimizations were achieved in Prompt 07, with Gemini 2.5 Pro judging model fixops41_gpt5_ops41_Combined_06...ipynb as fulfilling a) bi-directional digital twin requirements b) adequate external validation performance c) without critical flaws and inconsistencies seen in other notebooks. In Prompt 08, Gemini was utilized at Temp = 0.2, Top P = 0.9 to re-calibrate the model to standard and experimental arms by utilizing the prior QSP final trial code as a guide. Relevant ASME VV&40 and FDA relevant checks were obtained in a prompt utilizing the notebook and the prior QSP paper's VVUQ report as a template. Comparisons to prior notebooks were performed in Gemini to yield notebook Apply4d_VV40_100Arms_Vectoriza...ipynb that was closer to passing V&V tests than prior iterations.

Components of the notebook that were preventing further optimizations were refactored in Prompt 12 to obtain a simplified core dynamics model, functional toxicity/dropout model, and fixed disease progression and survival logic. The resulting functional notebook experienced run times that were slightly slower than prior versions utilizing JIT, parallelization, and vectorization. Additional Gemini comparisons were conducted amongst final versions of this tuned model, with a user optimized version of EMax and EC50 values chosen by Gemini, shown in Prompt 13 (g25p_Ref2d3USER_Apply4d_VV40...ipynb).
