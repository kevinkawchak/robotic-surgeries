# Results: Five Proposals

Each of the five proposals shown in ProposalsWordsMinutes were based on the six area reports generated by g25p. Proposal A by o3pr was the slowest at 13.16 minutes, while g25p was the fastest at 1.79 minutes. Word count was the shortest for grk3, and longest for g25p at 837 and 3,317 words respectively. Proposals were on average 1,781 words and took 4.46 minutes to generate by the AI models. Individual WPMs ranged from 99.6 for o3pr to 1,853.1 for g25p.

Prompt 63 in Standard F was obtained by meta-prompting o3pr with the prompt "For a startup seeking funding, what would be a good prompt to ask a future LLM for an effective: Prospective, end‑to‑end stand‑alone DT trial regarding the following 6 reports?" The resulting Prompt 63 was used to inform AI models that it was an "PDAC-DT-Architect," with a deep knowledge of "ASME V&V 40 credibility frameworks", "FDA/EMA regulatory pathways", and "pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma (PDAC) biology, therapeutics and the tumour micro-environment." Investor-ready proposals were informed to include 10 deliverables, which were observed across all generated proposals.

Each of the 5 proposals were labeled A-E and included as an input separately to the same five models, as seen in VerifyProposalsWordsMinutes. Run time performance followed a  similar pattern to the prior table, with o3pr having the slowest WPM at 38.15, while g25p had the fastest WPM at 1,290.38. Prompt 64 shown in Standard G in StandardG adds to Prompt 63 with verification instructions to "Rank the following Proposals A-E using a 6x5 formatted table scored 5.0-10.0" with specific headings used in subsequent visualizations: 1) Proposal 2) Report/MA Cited 3) Deliverable Completion 4) PDAC Trial Impact 5) Funding Probability.

By meta-prompting son4 with Dataset 2 containing the 5 proposals, 10 visualizations in Python were recommended. Of those, the most comprehensive diagram is shown in 5ProposalsRadar. Each proposal radar chart illustrates the four desired metrics previously requested in Prompt 64. Of particular interest for the average of 5 judge scores was Proposal A by o3pr, with a Deliverable Completion of 9.6 and a Funding Probability of 8.66. These results were followed in Proposal B by ops4, which had metrics of 8.86 and 8.58 in the same respective areas. Proposal D by o3ch followed Proposal B closely, and had an advantage over all models in PDAC Trial Impact at 8.94. Proposal C by g25p and Proposal E by grk3 had the least favorable Funding Probability of 6.74 and 5.62, as scored by the average across the five models. Overall performance across the five models followed a similar trend: o3pr > ops4 > o3ch > g25p > grk3. Tables generated separately in Python_08_Pro5Tab corresponded to these metrics.

# Results: Top 3 Proposals

Proposals A, B, and D by o3pr, ops4, and o3ch were found in the previous section to have the highest funding probability and also the highest overall averages. Each of the proposals utilizing the six report Dataset 2 correctly utilized the Daraxonrasib (pan-KRAS inhibitor), Mitazalimab (CD40 agonist), and Liposomal Irinotecan three drug combination determined to be have the highest predicted Therapeutic Synergy & Viability Score (TSVS) of 8.15 in Report 1 Table 2. These report findings are visualized in R1T23Drug_1, where four other 3 drug combinations were found to be largely inferior due to lower component scores in efficacy, toxicity, mechanism of action, and biomarker areas (TSVS = 7.02-7.40). The component breakdown for each of the 3 drug combinations is shown in the following R1T23Drug_2, with Daraxonrasib, Mitazalimab, and Liposomal Irinotecan having the highest percentages in MoA, Efficacy, and Toxicity.

## ChatGPT o3-pro

Proposal A by o3pr, with the highest chances of funding probability of 8.66/10, as averaged by the five models is shown in the paper. The proposal successfully addressed each of the 10 deliverables specified in Prompt 63. For instance, the 217 word Executive Summary provided a crisp value proposition for investors by detailing "24-month DT programme costs ≈ US$ 18 m versus US$ 70–90 m for a comparable wet-lab phase I/II" with a scope that "aligns with FDA Model-Informed Drug Development (MIDD) Pilot and EMA qualification advice pathways, de-risking acceptance of virtual evidence."

In addition, the sixth deliverable requirement consisting of Prospective DT Trial Protocol is to contain virtual eligibility criteria, treatment arms, endpoints, interim analyses, and decision rules. Proposal A provided substantial specifications in eligibility: "KRAS-mut PDAC, ECOG 0-1, adequate marrow/liver; excludes BRCAm & MSI-H (alternative SOC)", arms: "A) Triplet at DT-derived Recommended Starting Dose (RSD-1) ; B) Triplet at RSD-2 (20 % lower); C) Virtual SOC control (nal-IRI + 5-FU/LV)", primary endpoints: "Virtual Dose-Limiting Toxicity (vDLT) rate (cycle 1); Virtual Objective Response Rate (vORR) at 12 weeks", and also detailing interim analyses and decision rules.

The o3pr model finished the Deliverable Completion metric with the highest single score for any models at 9.60. Proposal A also had the highest score of 9.20 for Report/MA Cited, as averaged across the five models.

Several other report citations were included beyond the mentioned 3 drug combination for digital twin trial implementation:

- Bayesian hierarchical calibration using TSVS‑derived priors (Report 1) & CBS sequences (Report 3)
- "KRAS‑mutant GI tumours and for adaptive platform trials" (Report 5)
- CAF mechanotransduction rules from Reports 2 & 4
- "82 ODEs; humanised from Report 6 INT‑series parameters"
- Seven archetypes from Report 6 (ARCH‑01 to ARCH‑07) covering age, fitness, stage, KRAS alleles, BRCA/DDR status and stromal phenotypes.

The seven archetypes in Proposal A (ARCH‑01 to ARCH‑07) derived from Report 6 Table 1 are visualized using Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) values in R6ECOG. As an example, the digital twin trial would consist of a Young_Fit_Met archetype that is 45% ECOG 0 (Fully Active) and 55% ECOG 1 (Restricted), while the MET_KRAS_G12C archetype is 20% ECOG 0 (Fully Active) and 80% ECOG 1 (Restricted).
