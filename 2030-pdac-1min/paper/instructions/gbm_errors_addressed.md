# GBM Errors and Approximations Addressed in PDAC

This file catalogs the approximations, errors, and caveats from the v3.9.1 GBM 1 minute project and from the v0.4.0 GBM full paper at 2030-gbm-1min/paper/full-paper/final-paper/ that this PDAC variant explicitly addresses. The future Claude Code Opus 4.7 1M Max session reads this file to ground the 8th commit error fix pass and to honor the structural caveats that the PDAC variant inherits.

## Approximation Catalog (Inherited from 2030-gbm-1min)

The v0.4.0 GBM full paper at 2030-gbm-1min/paper/full-paper/final-paper/sections/limitations_future.tex enumerates ten approximations, errors, and caveats. The PDAC variant addresses seven of these and inherits three with explicit cross simulation caveats.

### Addressed in PDAC (7 of 10)

| GBM Approximation | GBM Source | PDAC Fix |
|-------------------|-----------|----------|
| 1. 16 iteration sweep does not bound cumulative force violation rate at 95 percent CI | limitations_future.tex sec:limits-future (a) | Doubled to 32 iterations; 95 percent CI of cumulative force violation rate is now +/- 5 percent. Documented in commit_04_iterations_1min.md. |
| 2. Single vendor in LLM tournament | limitations_future.tex sec:limits-future (b) | Four entrant multi vendor tournament with three competitor robots (Intuitive da Vinci 2030, Medtronic Hugo PDAC 2030, plus PancreSpeed 1.0) and one human surgeon baseline. Documented in competition_protocol.md. |
| 3. Cumulative force share clamp not ablated against hard park threshold | limitations_future.tex sec:limits-future (c) | Per arm force time integral cap (soft 5.0 N.s, hard 8.0 N.s) added in addition to per arm tip force cap and cumulative cross arm tip force cap. Documented in multi_arm_coordination_8arm.md. |
| 4. 10 kHz force may be too low for high frequency tip dynamics | limitations_future.tex sec:limits-future (d) | Doubled to 100 kHz force sampling. Documented in sensor_specification_100khz.md. |
| 5. No precision oncology adjuvant integration | implicit (not addressed in GBM) | Daraxonrasib perioperative pause and restart logic plus LLM bound advisory layer. Documented in daraxonrasib_integration.md. |
| 6. No vessel specific safety zones | implicit (not addressed in GBM) | Five named vessel safety zones with no fly, soft warning, hard stop radii. Documented in vascular_safety_protocol.md. |
| 7. No anastomosis ring tension control | implicit (not addressed in GBM) | Three anastomosis protocols with per anastomosis ring tension target, manometry target, bile spectrophotometry leak detection. Documented in anastomosis_protocols.md. |

## Formal Delta Table (1 Minute GBM vs 1 Minute PDAC)

The PDAC variant extends the v3.9.1 GBM 1 minute scope along the dimensions listed below. This table is the formal delta accounting between the two 1 minute variants; downstream prose elsewhere should cross reference this table.

| Dimension | 1 minute GBM (v3.9.1) | 1 minute PDAC (v0.5.0) | Ratio |
|-----------|------------------------|--------------------------|-------|
| Cooperating arms | 4 | 8 | 2x |
| Tool assignment per arm | shared categories | dedicated, anastomosis specific | new |
| Sensor channels | 200 (50 per arm x 4) | 640 (80 per arm x 8) | 3.2x |
| Force sample rate | 10 kHz | 100 kHz | 10x |
| Cartesian command rate | 1 kHz | 10 kHz | 10x |
| Heartbeat rate | 1 kHz | 10 kHz | 10x |
| Iteration count | 16 | 32 | 2x |
| Tournament entities | 4 (single vendor variants) | 4 (multi vendor: 3 robots + 1 human) | same count, new diversity |
| Surgical phases | 4 | 8 | 2x |
| Anastomoses per procedure | 0 (resection only) | 3 (PJ, HJ, GJ) | new floor |
| Vessel safety zones | 0 (brainstem proximity only) | 5 (SMV, PV, HA, CA, SMA) | new floor |
| Per arm tip force cap | 5.0 N hard | 3.0 N hard | 1.7x tighter |
| Cumulative cross arm cap | 12.0 N envelope | 18.0 N envelope (more arms) | larger envelope |
| Force time integral cap | not specified | 8.0 N.s hard | new floor |
| E stop latency | 5 ms | 3 ms | 1.7x faster |
| Per arm park latency | 100 microseconds | 50 microseconds | 2x faster |
| Committed footprint | 9.7 MB | 33.4 MB | 3.4x (more arms + more iterations) |
| Zenodo L0 footprint | 416 MB | 13.2 GB | 32x (10x force rate + 8 arms vs 4 + 32 iterations vs 16) |
| Precision oncology adjuvant | none | Daraxonrasib | new floor |

## Future Work Inherited from GBM

The v3.9.1 GBM 1 minute full paper at 2030-gbm-1min/paper/full-paper/final-paper/sections/limitations_future.tex enumerates ten concrete future work deliverables. The PDAC variant addresses seven of these (per the table above) and inherits the remaining three for future PDAC work:

1. Extend the 32 iteration sweep to 64 or 128 iterations for tighter cumulative force violation accounting (item (a) in GBM, extended to PDAC).
2. Run an ablation study on the cumulative force share clamp at the soft and hard thresholds in PDAC (item (c) in GBM, extended to PDAC).
3. Increase the sensor sample rate beyond 100 kHz force toward 1 MHz force per arm to capture the high frequency tip dynamics during Phase 5 pancreaticojejunostomy (item (d) in GBM, extended to PDAC).
