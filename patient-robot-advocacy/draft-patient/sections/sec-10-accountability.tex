\subsection{The question, stated as patients state it}

\draftinstr{Roughly 1,000 characters. The concern in the surveyed literature is
not that nobody is responsible; it is that everybody is, which amounts to the

hospital, the developer, and the manufacturer are all involved, and learns
nothing. Answer with the one-accountable-party rule and forward to Figure 29.}

\subsection{The audit trail, and your right to read it}

\draftinstr{Roughly 1,000 characters. Every model advisory, every gate decision,
every surgeon approval, and every safety-limit event is written to a
hash-chained record bound to a deterministic seed and a repository commit, under
21 CFR part 11 \cite{ecfr2026part11}. The participant may request their own
extract. State plainly what that extract will and will not contain, and state
that the chain cannot be edited after the fact, which is the property that makes
the record worth requesting. Cite \texttt{cfr312paiuni}.}
